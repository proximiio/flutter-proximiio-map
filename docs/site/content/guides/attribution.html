MapChrome.bare turns the credits off. Show them elsewhere in the app, for example in an about screen, when you use it.

A bottom sheet over the credits hides them. On iOS MapContentInsets.safeAreaPlus moves them above the sheet (see Camera and insets). On Android the insets do not move them; keep the bottom-right corner uncovered or show the credits elsewhere.

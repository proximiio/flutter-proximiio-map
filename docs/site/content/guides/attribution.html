The map shows the credits of the map data by default. Keep them visible and include the MapLibre BSD 2-Clause notice in the app's licences.

The controls

MapChromeDefault

MapChrome.bare turns the credits off. Show them elsewhere in the app, for example in an about screen, when you use it.

A bottom sheet over the credits hides them: keep the space free with MapContentInsets.safeAreaPlus instead (see Camera and insets).

Licences

The app ships MapLibre under the BSD 2-Clause licence. Add its notice to the app's licence screen, for example through Flutter's LicenseRegistry.

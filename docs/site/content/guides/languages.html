MapOptions.language sets the language of POI labels, floor names and guidance text.

ValueLanguage drawn
null (default)The app's language as the platform reports it
'ar', 'en', …That language, where the venue data has a translation
 
ProximiioMap(options: MapOptions(language: Localizations.localeOf(context).languageCode));

Rebuilding the widget with another language, or map.setOptions(...), redraws the labels.

Titles come from the venue data's translations (title_i18n), with the plain title as fallback. Guidance text exists in English and Arabic.
